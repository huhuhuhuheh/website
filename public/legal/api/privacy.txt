This is the boring legal part, so here is what actually happens with your data.

## the general API

- no account or key is needed for the general endpoints
- IP addresses are only used in memory for rate limiting, and are not written to the database
- no analytics, no ads, no tracking profiles, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone else

## the over the limits sync

- what gets stored: your minecraft uuid, the over limit values you sync (just numbers like bust size, offsets, cleavage, voice pitch, bounce and floppy multipliers), a hashed token that lets you update or delete your own data, and the time your config was last updated
- other players using the mod can see the values you synced, that is the whole point of the feature
- in most cases the server can also see that configuration, and if it runs the relay it can store it in its own database, depending on how that server secures it and where it is located
- you can delete your data at any time from the mod (opt out) or from the opt out page
- self hosted instances are run by whoever hosts them, and they are responsible for their own storage
